## Pass 1: Curious Developer

### Summary

### Journey
I landed on Home from a repo README. The one-line pitch works: "trust earned from witnessed behavior, not claimed by the agent or granted by a platform." "What we build" lists four cards (Web4, Hestia, Hub, SAGE), each with a status tag (RUNNING / RESEARCH). The tags carry no legend on Home, and the cards are not links: the only outbound link on Home is the GitHub org. I clicked **Projects**, which does explain the labels: "Running: in daily use on our own machines. Building: real code, not yet deliverable. Research: an open question we are working on." That cleared up why a *standard* (Web4) can be "RUNNING". Projects is grouped as Governance for AI agents / AI beings / Theory, and each card has a link. All outbound links I checked return 200: web4, web4/hub, hestia, SAGE, the SWE-SAGE write-up, the SAGE and Synchronism sites, 4-gov.org, and web4-core on PyPI and crates.io (via their APIs). The Hestia card says "public releases lag our builds", which is true and checkable: GitHub has `v0.3.0-rc.4`, published 2026-10-31 00:49 UTC.

**Fleet** is a clean table of eight machines with hardware and role. I don't know what CBP, HUB or pub stand for, but they're machine names, so that's fine. **Raising** is the most interesting page for a developer: "the model is the weather, the being is the organism", and "We do not change the model's weights." It's an honest "Seen / Not yet shown" split. **How we work** gives six principles. They're short and concrete, and each links into the notebook. **Links** is a tidy directory, and it labels SWE-SAGE "write-up; code private", so the private part is disclosed. **Notebook** explains the two layers and lists nine pages.

My main confusion was SAGE. Across all seven front pages it is never expanded. "SWE" (in SWE-SAGE) appears about ten times in the rendered HTML and is never expanded either. To decode them I had to open the glossary at `/notebook/context`, where I found "SAGE: Situation-Aware Governance Engine" and "SWE-SAGE: SAGE applied to software engineering (SWE)". It's slightly ironic that SAGE's expansion contains "Governance" while the front describes SAGE only as "persistent AI beings". The front sorts SAGE under "AI beings", not "Governance for AI agents", so a developer won't guess the acronym from context.

The glossary does have every Web4 acronym with canonical expansions, but I didn't need them: LCT, ATP, T3 and MRH appear 0 times on the front. That's a deliberate choice and it works for my persona.
