`/raising` was the page I expected to be fluff, and it isn't. It defines a "being" by what it has ("a name, a memory, a journal and a record of everything it has done"). I went looking for a "how do I run this" path. The front has none, which is fair for a summary, but "every repository … is in the notebook" on `/projects` links to `/notebook/projects`. The glossary is long, about 60 at-a-glance rows plus about 25 narrative sections in dependency order rather than alphabetical order, but it opens with "New here?
