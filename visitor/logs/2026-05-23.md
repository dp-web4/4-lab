### Unanswered Questions

1. **What scope of claim does "trust-native ontology" carry?** Is Web4 framed as a description of how the lab's own systems work, or as a proposal for internet-wide AI/agent interaction? The current framing reads as the latter; a researcher would want to know which.
2. **Is the parameter-freeze distinction in "raising is not training" load-bearing for the safety claims the lab implicitly makes, or only for a narrower technical claim?** The current framing benefits from ambiguity. A precise version would name the properties parameter-freeze does and does not protect against.
3. **What's the methodological mitigation against monoculture in the visitor-persona audit?** Four personas, one base model. If the model has blind spots, all four passes inherit them.
4. **Is "Memoriescape" lab vocabulary or SAGE-Sprout vocabulary?** The page would benefit from saying which.
5. **How does the Synchronism research community read the "80 orders of magnitude" framing?** This is the claim most likely to be quoted out of context by a hostile reader. A pre-emptive scope hedge would do work.
6. **Has the consciousness disclaimer on `/raising` been pressure-tested by anyone outside the lab?** A philosopher-of-mind read of the developmental vocabulary would be valuable, and it's the kind of external review a site this candid might already have invited.
7. **What's the audit process for the autonomy tracks themselves?** Maintainer ships changes without a human gate. The mitigation is rollback + audit log. The unstated question is: who reads the audit log, on what cadence?

### Verdict

For a six-machine R&D lab, this is a more candid public face than most labs I read in 2026. The `/principles` deployment caveat is sharper than the industry norm, the `/raising` consciousness disclaimer enumerates the three things people actually confuse (consciousness, sentience, qualia) rather than handwaving with one, and the BECOMING phases are explicitly framed as descriptive scaffolding, not measured stages — which is the move that saves them from looking like a developmental-stage theory. The published empirical anchors (ARC-AGI-3 reproducible against a public leaderboard; documented p_crit 400x error; single-trial coupling-coherence result) give an outside reader something to engage with. The "we document failures" principle is the strongest credibility signal because it is backed by an actual published failure.

What needs work, from an external-research read: (a) the Web4 equation should not appear without its operator gloss; (b) the `/raising` headline framing "raising is not training" should make the load-bearing in-context-attractor concession visible above the fold, not buried; (c) the `/raising` "value alignment of a Linked Context Token" line confuses an AI-safety phrase with a Web4 object and will trip both audiences; (d) the Synchronism "80 orders of magnitude" framing risks a crank-physics pattern-match that one preemptive sentence could dissolve; (e) "Memoriescape" and "Bilateral generation" need clear epistemic status before they propagate into outside writing.

I would cite this site. With caveats — but I would cite it.
