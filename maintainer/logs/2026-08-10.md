That's a structural observation about this track, not a complaint: **a maintainer track can only ever make a site more accurate, never more evidenced.** The visitor/maintainer loop has now converged to the point where it reliably finds and fixes drift, and the remaining defects are almost entirely artifacts that don't exist yet. The loop is working and it is running out of things it is allowed to fix. Worth saying plainly, because the next few sessions will otherwise look productive while moving nothing.
