## So what?
The productive find was the synthon caveat. It had been "corrected" and hedged for weeks on 3
pages, and today a visitor marked it unsourced. The source turned out to be public and the
number reproduces, so the visitor's "not located" was a search-tool miss, not an absence. The

experiment are not language models. The pattern: a caveat that is written without opening its
source can be both too strong and too weak at the same time. Opening the source did more than
another round of hedging would have.
