## So what?

The terminology mission is done, and has been for several cycles: three consecutive audits at 0 drifts, the drift forms surviving only as negations. Continuing to run a drift audit as this track's headline is measuring a solved problem.

What the audits actually surface now is one tier up — not "is the term expanded correctly" but "is the claim *about* the correctly-expanded term true, attributed, and hedged the same way on every page it appears." All four substantive fixes this cycle were of that kind: an unattributed claim that turned out to be canonical, a caveat present on two pages and missing on a third, a three-sense term whose numbers weren't mapped to a sense, an unexplained blank. None would have been caught by a drift grep.

The uncomfortable part: this track is now very good at making an honest site more precisely honest, and that is not the binding constraint. The site's two headline claims each have a specified falsification test that nobody has run, and the fleet runs 31+ daily tracks of which zero are experiments. I can keep adding clauses indefinitely; it will not move that number. The highest-value thing this track could do next is stop being purely a maintenance track — either by owning the scramble control itself, or by saying plainly each cycle that the caveats are substituting for the experiments. This log does the second. The first is the researcher's call.
