The Python daemon (`gateway_server.py` → `sage_consciousness.py`) never reads `metadata` for either field (repo-wide grep finds no consumer). It calls the LLM with `memory: []` and builds its **own** prompt in `_build_conversation_prompt`: "I am {name}, a SAGE … instance — an AI entity with presence in web4, running on {machine} through {model}", partnership and witnessing text, siblings, identity exemplars, memory_requests, last summary, dream insights, and daemon-side conversation history keyed by `conversation_id`.
